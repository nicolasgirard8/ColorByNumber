# Listing — English

**Name**: Pixel Coloriage Kids

**Short description (80 chars max)**:
Pixel color-by-number for little ones: dinos, trucks, soccer, hockey and more!

**Full description**:
Pixel Coloriage Kids is a color-by-number game made for children aged 3 to 8.
Pick a color, tap the squares with the same number, and watch the picture appear pixel by pixel!

🦖 150+ original pictures: dinosaurs, fire trucks, police, space, ocean, soccer, hockey…
👆 Big buttons and big squares for small fingers
🔍 Pinch to zoom, plus XXL pictures for older kids
🪣 Paint bucket and color bomb to fill faster
📒 Sticker album: every finished picture joins the collection
👨‍👩‍👦 A profile for each child with their own progress
📷 Turn a photo into a coloring page

No ads, no in-app purchases, no internet, no data collection.
Everything stays on the device.
